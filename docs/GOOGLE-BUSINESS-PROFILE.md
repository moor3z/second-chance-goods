# Google Business Profile: checklist

Nothing has been created or published. Work through this before deciding whether to set up a profile.

## 1. Check eligibility first
Google's guidelines only allow a Business Profile for businesses that meet customers **in person** during
stated hours: a shopfront or premises customers can visit, or a service-area business that travels to
customers. **Online-only businesses are not eligible.** An eBay shop with no customer-facing premises or
in-person service probably doesn't qualify on its own. Confirm against Google's current guidelines.

Confirm which applies to Second Chance Goods Ltd:
- [ ] Customers can visit premises (e.g. a shop, or collection from a staffed address during set hours)
- [ ] You travel to customers (e.g. collecting or delivering items in person across an area)
- [ ] Neither: online/eBay only (not eligible; use the website and eBay shop for search visibility instead)

## 2. Details to confirm (only if eligible)
- [ ] Exact business name as used with customers (Google requires the real-world name, no keyword stuffing)
- [ ] Primary category (e.g. "Second hand store" or "Used furniture store", whichever is accurate)
- [ ] Address customers visit, **or** service areas if you hide the address
- [ ] Opening hours (only hours you're genuinely staffed)
- [ ] Phone number and email you'll answer
- [ ] Website link: your final domain (not the `pages.dev` address)
- [ ] Short description in your own words, matching the website's About page
- [ ] Verification method Google offers you (video, postcard, phone) and who will do it

## 3. Branding
- [ ] Logo: `public/assets/img/logo.svg` (export a square PNG of the chair mark for the profile photo)
- [ ] Cover photo: a real photo of your stock or premises, not the placeholder hero image
- [ ] A handful of genuine photos of items and, if applicable, premises

## 4. Things that don't happen automatically
- The eBay sync updates **this website only**. It does not update Google products, Google Merchant Center
  or a Business Profile.
- eBay seller feedback is not a Google review and must not be presented as one.
- If you later want products on Google Shopping, that's a separate Merchant Center setup with its own
  policies about where checkout happens; check eligibility before building anything.

## 5. Website details still needed
The site deliberately shows no address, phone number, company number or opening hours, and has no
LocalBusiness structured data. Once you confirm real customer-facing details, they can be added.
